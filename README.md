## 📌 Overview

**RESQ** is an AI-powered emergency medical response and coordination platform designed to improve the speed, efficiency, and coordination of emergency healthcare services.

Initially focused on the **Kerala healthcare ecosystem**, RESQ aims to create a connected emergency-response network linking:

**Citizens → Emergency Responders → Ambulances → Hospitals**

The platform combines **AI-assisted triage, ambulance tracking, route assistance, hospital pre-alerts, and real-time communication** into a unified emergency-response workflow.

> 🚧 **Status:** Prototype / Under Development

---

## 🎯 Vision

To build a faster, smarter, and more connected emergency healthcare ecosystem where critical information reaches the right responders and hospitals at the right time.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Emergency medical response can be affected by:

- Delays in ambulance dispatch
- Limited visibility of available ambulances
- Communication gaps between responders and hospitals
- Traffic and route-related challenges
- Delayed hospital preparation
- Fragmented emergency information
- Difficulty prioritizing emergency cases

RESQ is designed to address these challenges through a centralized, intelligent, and real-time coordination platform.
